UltraModern Technologies Pvt. Ltd. — Privacy Policy

When someone fills out a contact form or sends us an enquiry, they are trusting us with their information. We do not take that lightly. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who can see it, and what you can do about it.

We are UltraModern Technologies Pvt. Ltd., an IT services company based in Indore, India. We build websites, software, and mobile apps, run digital marketing and SEO campaigns, design UI/UX, develop CRM systems, deliver AI and machine learning solutions, and provide IT consulting and cloud services. Our clients are spread across India and internationally.

One thing worth stating plainly: we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Not to anyone, not for any reason. Using our website or getting in touch means you are agreeing to what is described here.

01 · Data Collection

What We Collect

What you share with us

When you fill out a contact form, request a quote, subscribe to our newsletter, or call or email us, you may share your name, job title, email address, phone number, company name, website URL, and project details.

What we collect automatically

Like most websites, ours picks up certain data passively when you visit: your IP address and approximate location, browser type and operating system, referring and exit pages, which pages you viewed and for how long, device type and screen size, and session timestamps.

We do not collect financial details, government ID numbers, biometric data, or health information through this website.

02 · Data Usage

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for the following purposes:

  • Responding to you: Your contact details and message help us give you a useful, timely reply.
  • Project delivery: For clients who hire us, the information you provide helps us scope, plan, and deliver the work.
  • Improving the site: Aggregated analytics data shows us what is working and what is not.
  • Marketing communications: If you have opted in, we may send occasional updates about our services. Every email includes an unsubscribe link.
  • Legal compliance: Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and related rules, requires us to retain or process certain data.
  • Security: Log data and IP addresses help us identify unusual activity and protect the site.

The basis for each of these is your consent, a legitimate business interest, or a legal obligation, depending on the context.

03 · Cookie Policy

Cookies

  • Essential cookies are required for the site to function, managing session security and similar tasks. You cannot opt out of these while using the site.
  • Functional cookies remember your preferences, such as language or region, to keep your experience consistent on return visits.
  • Analytics cookies, primarily set by Google Analytics, help us understand how visitors move through the site. The data is aggregated and does not identify you individually.
  • Advertising cookies are set by Google Ads when we run campaigns. They help us show relevant ads to previous visitors and measure whether those ads are generating enquiries.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block, limit, or clear them. Disabling certain cookies may affect how the site functions.

04 · Analytics

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how people use our site. GA4 sends data, via cookies, to Google servers that may be located outside India. The data includes pages visited, time on site, traffic source, device type, and approximate location. We have enabled IP anonymisation, so the last portion of your IP address is masked before reaching Google.

We do not link GA4 data to any personally identifiable information you submit through our contact forms.

To opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites, you can install Google's browser add-on. Google's full privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

05 · Advertising

Google Ads & Remarketing

We run Google Ads campaigns, including remarketing. If you have visited our site, you may see our ads on other websites within Google's Display Network. This works through a cookie placed on your browser when you visit us.

We do not upload your personal details to Google Ads without your explicit consent. What we see on our end is campaign-level, aggregated data only.

To opt out of personalised Google advertising, visit adssettings.google.com or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page at optout.networkadvertising.org.

06 · Social Platforms

Social Media

Our site may include sharing buttons or links to LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, Twitter/X, and YouTube. When you interact with those, the relevant platform may set its own cookies or collect data, even if you are not logged in at the time. We have no control over that.

Their privacy policies:

  • LinkedIn: linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram): facebook.com/policy.php
  • Twitter/X: twitter.com/en/privacy
  • Google (YouTube): policies.google.com/privacy

If you message us through social media, we use only your username and message content to respond. We do not import social media contact lists into our own systems.

07 · Vendors

Third-Party Services

  • Hosting: Our site runs on a cloud infrastructure provider. Server logs, which may include IP addresses and browser data, are kept for a limited period for security and performance purposes.
  • Email: Client communications are stored on our mail servers and accessible only to authorised staff.
  • CRM and project tools: Client information may be stored in our CRM system. Access is restricted to staff who need it, and data is retained only as long as the business relationship requires.
  • Payments: We do not process payments through this website. Any financial transaction goes through a separately disclosed payment gateway. We do not store card or banking details on our servers.

All third-party providers are contractually required to process data only as we instruct and to maintain appropriate security standards.

08 · Data Sharing

Who We Share Your Information With

We share data only in these situations:

  • Project delivery: Relevant information may be shared with freelancers or development partners working on your project. All are under confidentiality agreements.
  • Technology providers: Analytics, email, and cloud platforms may process limited data as described in section 7.
  • Legal requirements: If Indian law, a court order, or a regulatory authority requires disclosure, we will comply, and where legally permitted, we will notify you beforehand.
  • Business transfers: If UltraModern Technologies is ever acquired or merges with another company, your data may transfer as part of that process. The acquiring entity would be bound by the same commitments described in this policy.
  • With your consent: Anything outside the above, we will ask you first.

We have never sold personal data and have no intention of doing so.

09 · Protection

Data Security

Our site uses HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data transmitted between your browser and our servers. Access to stored personal data is limited to authorised staff on a need-to-know basis. We apply regular security updates. CRM data is access-controlled and audit-logged.

No internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure. If you believe your interaction with us has been compromised, contact us immediately at ultramoderntechnologies01@gmail.com.

10 · Storage

Data Retention

We hold onto information only for as long as there is a genuine reason to. Contact form submissions are reviewed and cleared on a rolling basis. Project-related data may be kept for the duration of the engagement and a reasonable period afterward for legal and reference purposes. Analytics data may be retained in anonymised form.

When there is no longer a purpose for keeping something, we delete or anonymise it.

11 · Your Control

Your Rights

Regardless of where you are based, you can:

  • Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Ask us to delete your data, subject to any legal retention requirements
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time
  • Ask us to stop using your data for direct marketing
  • Request your data in a portable, machine-readable format where feasible

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 17. We will respond within 30 days. There is no charge for reasonable requests.

12 · Minors

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

If you believe a minor has submitted information to us, contact us at ultramoderntechnologies01@gmail.com and we will investigate promptly and delete any such data if confirmed.

13 · Cross-Border

International Data Transfers

We are based in India and most of our data processing occurs here. However, some third-party tools we use, including Google Analytics, Google Ads, and our cloud infrastructure, may store or process data on servers in other countries, including the United States and the European Economic Area.

Where data is transferred internationally, we rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms, along with contractual requirements that all providers maintain security standards at least equal to our own. If you have concerns about international transfers, please reach out and we will address them directly.

14 · Limitation

Disclaimer of Absolute Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

Any data you transmit to us is done at your own risk, and we encourage sensible precautions on your end as well.

15 · Jurisdiction

Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of India. Any disputes arising from this policy or our data practices fall under the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Indore, Madhya Pradesh.

16 · Changes

Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy as our practices change, as new legal requirements come in, or as we find clearer ways to explain things. The Effective Date at the top reflects when the current version took effect.

For changes that materially affect how your data is handled, we will try to notify you directly where we have your contact details, or post a clear notice on the site. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

17 · Get in Touch

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, want to exercise your data rights, or have a concern about how we have handled your information, please reach out. We will respond within 30 days.
