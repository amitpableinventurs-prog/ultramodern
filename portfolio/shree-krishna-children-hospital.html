Overview

About the project

A modern website for Shree Krishna Children Hospital in Bhopal, a multi-speciality hospital with paediatric specialists, that helps families find the right department and doctor and book an appointment.

The challenge

Parents looking for paediatric care need quick answers: which specialist to see, whether emergency care is available, and how to book. The hospital needed a site that gives those answers in a few taps.

Our solution

A fast single-page web app with department and doctor pages, a 24/7 emergency call action, and appointment booking from every screen.

Key features

  • Department pages for every speciality
  • Doctor profiles with qualifications
  • Book-appointment and emergency call actions
  • Patient care journey and testimonials
  • Mobile-first design
