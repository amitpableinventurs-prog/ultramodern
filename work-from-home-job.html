How We Work

How We Manage Remote Teams

Written Communication First

Project updates, decisions, and feedback in writing. Creates a record, gives time to think, works across time zones.

Defined Availability Windows

4–6 hour overlap with Indore core team. Work from anywhere in India without a rigid 9-to-6 schedule.

Sprint-Based Delivery

Weekly or biweekly sprints. Managed by what got done, not hours logged.

Regular Video Check-ins

2–3 times per week for most teams. Monthly one-on-ones with your lead.

Onboarding with a Buddy

New remote hires paired with a team member from same department for first month.
